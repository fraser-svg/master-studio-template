# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
Two customer modes, both served by the same page:
1. **Urgent** — something is broken now. On a phone, one-handed, often outdoors or stressed. Job: reach a human fast and believe they will actually turn up.
2. **Planning** — a considered job worth real money. On desktop or tablet, comparing two or three quotes. Job: judge competence and risk from photos, reviews, accreditations and a clear process before booking a survey.

Secondary: the business owner, who edits nothing in code — every business-specific string is a GoHighLevel Custom Value in `src/lib/site.ts`.

### Brand Personality
Six words: **masculine, tough, practical, trustworthy, plain, established.**

This is a firm that has been doing the work for twenty years and does not need to shout about it. Tone: a good tradesperson explaining the job at the kitchen table — plain, specific, no jargon, no salesy inflation. Numbers over adjectives ("14 years, 380 roofs" beats "market-leading"). Short words, short sentences, roughly 5th-grade reading level. Emotional goal: relief for the urgent user, confidence for the planner. Never: corporate, cute, startup-y, apologetic, or clever.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Three styles, one contract.** The template ships three complete designs, chosen
per client by the `site_style` Custom Value and applied as `data-style` on `<html>`:

| | `steel` (default) | `ink` | `board` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | warm concrete | warm paper | white |
| Display | Anton | Archivo Narrow 700 | Archivo Black |
| Body | Archivo | Source Serif 4 | Archivo |
| Accent | signal orange, actions only | burnt sienna, marks only | saturated yellow field |
| Rules | 1px hairline | 1px hairline | 2px / 4px hard |
| Reads as | a firm with a yard | a firm that charges more | loud and memorable |

These are **not** the three `site_theme` palettes deleted in the 2026-09-10 audit.
Those were unused colour swaps that phase 5 repainted over. These are drawn,
reviewed designs that differ in type, rule weight, band rhythm and colour stance.
/studio phase 3 picks one and records why in `DESIGN.md`; phase 5 repaints the
brand hue *inside* it. Do not delete them as dead weight.

Every difference between the three lives in `:root[data-style="..."]` in
`src/styles.css`. A component must never branch on the style, and
`grep -rn 'siteStyle\|data-style' src/components/` must return nothing.

Whichever style is active, the work is heavy and built, not decorated. Weight comes
from the type and the blocks, never from effects.

- **One loud moment per page.** The display face opts in through `.display`; nothing
  is uppercase or heavy by default. Exactly ONE element per page reaches
  `--text-step-4`, and it is the h1. When every heading shouts, nothing is a
  hierarchy and the page reads cheap - that is the mistake this template shipped
  with before 2026-09-10.
- **Bands, not tints.** The page is a SEQUENCE of full-bleed solid bands built with
  `<Band tone size>`: light, alt, deep and dark, with at least three dark bands per
  page and the vertical size varying so the page compresses and expands. A band tone
  never takes an opacity - `bg-secondary/40` against `bg-background` is a 0.04
  lightness difference nobody can see, and it is what made the whole page one flat
  field. No floating cards on grey. No shadows. No gradients. No glass.
- **Hard edges.** Thin 1px borders, small radii (0.25rem). Things look machined, not soft.
- **Real photography of real work.** Vans, sites, finished jobs, faces of the actual crew. Never stock.
- **Restraint in colour.** One dark brand colour, one warm neutral, one accent used sparingly — buttons, active states, small marks. Never three competing colours.

**Tokens, and only tokens.** A client site is re-skinned by replacing token values
in the active style's `:root` block - nothing else. Components must never hard-code
a colour, a rule weight, a font or a band padding; if a component needs a value that
is not a token, the token is missing. Add it to all three style blocks.

One trap, and the reason `.display` and the `rule-*` utilities exist: Tailwind
compiles `@theme` entries into utility classes with the value **inlined at build
time**, so a `:root[data-style]` override never reaches them. `font-display` and
`border` cannot carry a style-varying token. Colour tokens are safe - they resolve
through `var()` at runtime, which is why the repaint test works.

Each style's palette is a **starting point**, not a brand. Every client build
repaints it from their real scraped colours.

Light-first. No dark mode unless a client build validates one.

**Anti-references:** SaaS landing pages. Purple/blue gradients. Glassmorphism. Big rounded cards floating on light grey. Stock smiling-people-in-hardhats. Gradient text. Emoji as icons. Pulsing "available now" dots. Pill clutter. Equal three-card rows. Hero stat rows of three round invented numbers. Low-contrast dimmed white text.

### Design Principles
1. **The phone number is the product.** Contact is reachable within one thumb-move from any scroll position on mobile, on every page.
2. **Proof over promise.** Every claim sits next to something verifiable — a photo, a number, an accreditation, a named review. Cut any sentence that could appear on a competitor's site unchanged.
3. **Tokens or it doesn't ship.** Zero hard-coded colours, rule weights or fonts in
   components. Repainting the active style's `:root` block and reloading is the
   test, and it must pass in all three styles.
4. **Structure carries the design.** Impact comes from type scale, rhythm and section contrast, not from decoration, shadows or effects.
5. **Generic is the failure mode.** This template will be deployed dozens of times;
   it must not look like a template, and it must not look AI-made. Two checks say
   more than four screenshot reviews: the homepage's sections must render **at least
   three distinct band backgrounds including a dark one**, and exactly **one**
   element per page at `--text-step-4`.

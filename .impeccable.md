# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
Two homeowner modes, both must be served by the same page:
1. **Urgent** — something is broken now (leak, blocked drain, unsafe electrics). On a phone, one-handed, often outdoors or stressed. Job: reach a human fast and believe they will actually turn up.
2. **Planning** — a considered project (extension, re-roof, full rewire, driveway). On desktop or tablet, comparing two or three quotes. Job: judge competence and risk from photos, reviews, accreditations and a clear process before booking a survey.

Secondary: the trade business owner, who edits nothing in code — every business-specific string is a GoHighLevel Custom Value in `src/lib/site.ts`.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **competent, local, straight-talking.**
Tone: a good tradesperson explaining the job at the kitchen table — plain, specific, no jargon and no salesy inflation. Numbers over adjectives ("14 years, 380 roofs" beats "market-leading"). Emotional goal: relief for the urgent user, confidence for the planner. Never: corporate, cute, startup-y, or apologetic.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial trade — heavy condensed display type, real work photography, sand/warm-neutral panels alternating with a deep brand colour, thin borders, small radii (0.25rem), flat surfaces.

**Themeable by design.** One structure, several token themes a client picks from. All colour, radius and font decisions live as CSS custom properties in `src/styles.css`; components must never hard-code a colour. A theme swap must change the entire site's character without touching a single component. Ship at least: Forest (current), Industrial (concrete grey + safety orange), Premium (near-black + warm metallic).

Light-first. Dark mode only if every theme has a validated dark variant.

**Anti-references:** SaaS landing pages. Purple/blue gradients. Glassmorphism. Big rounded cards floating on a light-grey page. Stock smiling-people-in-hardhats. Gradient text. Emoji as icons. Hero stat rows of three round numbers.

### Design Principles
1. **The phone number is the product.** Contact is reachable within one thumb-move from any scroll position on mobile, on every page.
2. **Proof over promise.** Every claim sits next to something verifiable — a photo, a number, an accreditation, a named review. Cut any sentence that could appear on a competitor's site unchanged.
3. **Tokens or it doesn't ship.** Zero hard-coded colours in components. Theme swap is the test.
4. **Structure carries the design.** Impact comes from type scale, rhythm and section contrast, not from decoration, shadows or effects.
5. **Generic is the failure mode.** This template will be deployed dozens of times; it must not look like a template, and it must not look AI-made.

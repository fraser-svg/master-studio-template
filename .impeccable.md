# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
Two customer modes, both served by the same page:
1. **Urgent** — something is broken now. On a phone, one-handed, often outdoors or stressed. Job: reach a human fast and believe they will actually turn up.
2. **Planning** — a considered job worth real money. On desktop or tablet, comparing two or three quotes. Job: judge competence and risk from photos, reviews, accreditations and a clear process before booking a survey.

Secondary: the business owner, who edits nothing in code — every business-specific string is a GoHighLevel Custom Value in `src/lib/site.ts`.

### Brand Personality
Six words: **masculine, tough, practical, trustworthy, plain, established.**

This is a firm that has been doing the work for twenty years and does not need to shout about it. Tone: a good tradesperson explaining the job at the kitchen table — plain, specific, no jargon, no salesy inflation. Numbers over adjectives ("14 years, 380 roofs" beats "market-leading"). Short words, short sentences, roughly 5th-grade reading level. Emotional goal: relief for the urgent user, confidence for the planner. Never: corporate, cute, startup-y, apologetic, or clever.

### Aesthetic Direction
Heavy and built, not decorated. Weight comes from the type and the blocks, never from effects.

- **Type does the shouting.** Condensed uppercase display face at large sizes against small, quiet body text. Big jumps between steps in the scale — a timid type scale is what makes a site look generic.
- **Solid flat blocks.** Full-bleed bands of one colour, edge to edge, alternating light and brand-dark. No floating cards on grey. No shadows. No gradients. No glass.
- **Hard edges.** Thin 1px borders, small radii (0.25rem). Things look machined, not soft.
- **Real photography of real work.** Vans, sites, finished jobs, faces of the actual crew. Never stock.
- **Restraint in colour.** One dark brand colour, one warm neutral, one accent used sparingly — buttons, active states, small marks. Never three competing colours.

**One theme.** There is a single palette, in `:root` in `src/styles.css`. A client site is re-skinned by replacing those values and the two font families — nothing else. Components must never hard-code a colour; if a component needs a colour that is not a token, the token is missing. Add it.

The base palette is a **neutral starting point**, not a brand. Every client build repaints it from their real scraped colours.

Light-first. No dark mode unless a client build validates one.

**Anti-references:** SaaS landing pages. Purple/blue gradients. Glassmorphism. Big rounded cards floating on light grey. Stock smiling-people-in-hardhats. Gradient text. Emoji as icons. Pulsing "available now" dots. Pill clutter. Equal three-card rows. Hero stat rows of three round invented numbers. Low-contrast dimmed white text.

### Design Principles
1. **The phone number is the product.** Contact is reachable within one thumb-move from any scroll position on mobile, on every page.
2. **Proof over promise.** Every claim sits next to something verifiable — a photo, a number, an accreditation, a named review. Cut any sentence that could appear on a competitor's site unchanged.
3. **Tokens or it doesn't ship.** Zero hard-coded colours in components. Repainting `:root` and reloading is the test.
4. **Structure carries the design.** Impact comes from type scale, rhythm and section contrast, not from decoration, shadows or effects.
5. **Generic is the failure mode.** This template will be deployed dozens of times; it must not look like a template, and it must not look AI-made.
